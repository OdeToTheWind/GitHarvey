# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), summarised here.

## Our pledge

We want everyone to take part in a harassment-free way, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient and welcoming.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully. Assume good intent.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people who use it.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention
- Publishing someone else's private information, including their GitHub token
- Anything else that would be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code of conduct, and may ban people who break it.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile ([@OdeToTheWind](https://github.com/OdeToTheWind)) or send a private security report if it involves a security problem. Reports are handled privately.
